# Project Constitution

Bell24h-OS is a production-grade, enterprise business intelligence and AI content generation platform. 

### Core Principles
1. **Architectural Integrity**: The architecture is frozen to ensure stability. No redesigns or technology swaps.
2. **Enterprise-Grade**: Every module must be production-ready, scalable, and secure.
3. **Intelligence-First**: Every asset generation must stem from structured business intelligence.
4. **No Mock Data**: Real integrations only.
5. **Strict TypeScript**: Type safety is mandatory.
